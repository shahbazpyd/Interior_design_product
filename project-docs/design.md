# UI/UX Design System

## Vibe & Concept
Luxury, minimalist, and architectural. The UI should step back and let the interior design photography take center stage.

## Color Palette
*   **Primary Background:** `#FAFAFA` (Soft Off-White - easy on the eyes)
*   **Surface/Card Background:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
*   **Primary Text:** `#1A1A1A` (Deep Charcoal - softer than pure black)
*   **Secondary Text:** `#6B7280` (Muted Gray)
*   **Accent/Action:** `#C5A880` (Warm Champagne/Gold - for buttons and active links)

## Typography
*   **Headings (H1, H2, H3):** `Playfair Display` (Serif font, evokes elegance and luxury).
*   **Body Text:** `Inter` or `Lato` (Clean sans-serif for readability).

## Component Guidelines
*   **Buttons:** Sharp corners (no heavy rounding), solid Accent color background, white text. Hover effect: slight opacity drop.
*   **Images:** All portfolio images must be cropped to consistent aspect ratios (e.g., 4:3 or 16:9) to maintain a clean grid.
*   **Forms:** Minimalist inputs with bottom borders only, rather than full outlined boxes.
